Risotto

A creamy and flavorful Italian rice dish made by slowly cooking Arborio rice in broth until it reaches a rich, velvety consistency. Finished with Parmesan cheese and seasonings, risotto is a comforting meal that can be enjoyed on its own or served as a side dish.

Ingredients

  • Arborio rice
  • Chicken or vegetable broth
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Butter
  • Olive oil
  • White wine (optional)
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the broth in a separate saucepan and keep it warm.
  2. Heat olive oil and butter in a large pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the chopped onion and cook until soft.
  4. Add the Arborio rice and stir for 1–2 minutes until the grains are lightly toasted.
  5. Pour in the white wine, if using, and stir until it is absorbed.
  6. Add the warm broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently and allowing each addition to absorb before adding more.
  7. Continue cooking for about 18–20 minutes until the rice is creamy and tender.
  8. Stir in the Parmesan cheese and remaining butter.
  9. Season with salt and black pepper.
  10. Serve immediately while hot and creamy.
