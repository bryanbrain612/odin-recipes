Cheesyplantain

Golden, ripe plantains baked or fried until tender and caramelized, then topped with melted cheese for a delicious combination of sweet and savory flavors. This simple and satisfying dish makes a great snack, side dish, or light meal.

Ingredients

  • Ripe plantains
  • Cheddar or mozzarella cheese
  • Butter or cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Peel the ripe plantains and slice them lengthwise or into thick pieces.
  3. Place the plantain slices on a baking tray and brush them lightly with butter or cooking oil.
  4. Season with a small amount of salt and black pepper if desired.
  5. Bake for 15–20 minutes, turning halfway through, until golden and tender.
  6. Remove the plantains from the oven and sprinkle the shredded cheese evenly over them.
  7. Return to the oven and bake for another 3–5 minutes until the cheese has melted.
  8. Serve warm as a snack, side dish, or light meal.
