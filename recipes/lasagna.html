Lasagna

A classic Italian comfort food made with layers of tender pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned ground beef, and creamy melted cheese. Baked until golden and bubbling, this hearty dish is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
