Lasagna

A classic Italian comfort food made with layers of tender pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned ground beef, and creamy melted cheese. Baked until golden and bubbling, this hearty dish is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned. Season with salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  4. Pour in the tomato sauce and let it simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  5. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish, followed by noodles, ricotta cheese, mozzarella, and Parmesan.
  7. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  8. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes.
  9. Remove the foil and bake for another 10–15 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling.
  10. Let the lasagna rest for a few minutes before serving.
